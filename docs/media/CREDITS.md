# Media credits

All screenshots, artwork and trailer footage come from **Solbound** itself; the trailer footage was recorded from real gameplay. The trailer was edited for **LoversGG**.

## Trailer music

The trailer uses an original score and sound design made for this trailer. It contains no third-party music.

## In-game music and sound effects

The game's music (four looping pieces: title theme, Moon surface, Mars surface, homecoming) and its sound effects (launch, landing, decision chime, alarm, results, forecast blips) are original compositions made for Solbound. They are synthesised live in the browser with the Web Audio API (`src/audio/sound.ts`): there are no audio files and no third-party music or samples.
